# Public disclosure boundary

> **CONFIDENTIAL — PRIVATE RESEARCH — NOT FOR PUBLIC RELEASE**

## Purpose

This document distinguishes previously public work from the new
confidential PETRA VISION concept.

It is a research record, not a legal opinion.

## Previously public subject matter

Public PET and PETRA materials historically included topics such as:

- prime-exponent and recursive integer structures;
- canonical structural representations;
- structural metrics;
- positional addresses;
- native structural operators;
- deterministic serialisation and roundtrip behaviour;
- experimental integration with compression tooling.

Public Onion Compressor Framework materials included PETVISION2 and
PETVISION3 experiments concerning lossless preprocessing and compact
binary representation of factorable integer-line payloads.

Those PETVISION experiments used concepts such as prime dictionaries,
shape dictionaries, record kinds, and reversible binary transforms.

## New confidential subject matter

The present confidential work begins with a distinct concept:

- a PETRA shape is mapped directly to canonical spatial geometry;
- the spatial geometry itself embodies the recursive structure;
- the original native shape is reconstructed from that geometry;
- structural relations correspond to geometric relations;
- recursive semantic levels may be exposed at multiple scales;
- incomplete geometry may be completed through an authenticated
  external contribution.

## Explicit distinction

The confidential PETRA VISION concept is not defined merely by:

- the previous name PETVISION;
- compact binary factorisation formats;
- drawing an ordinary tree;
- serialising PETRA data into pixels;
- placing PETRA text or bytes inside a QR-like carrier;
- visualising metrics without reversible structural correspondence.

## Open prior-art questions

A professional search must investigate at least:

- canonical graph and tree drawing;
- reversible diagrammatic representations;
- recursive and fractal coding;
- visual cryptography;
- machine-readable geometric markers;
- multiresolution symbolic systems;
- topological and spatial data encoding;
- geometric hashing and structural fingerprints;
- authenticated partial-image completion;
- graph-to-geometry embeddings with exact inverse reconstruction.

## Conservative working rule

No assumption of novelty should be made merely because an identical
name or identical implementation has not yet been found.

Novelty and inventive step must be evaluated against the substance
of the technical method.
