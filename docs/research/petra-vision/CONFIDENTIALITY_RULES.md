# PETRA VISION confidentiality rules

> **CONFIDENTIAL — PRIVATE RESEARCH — NOT FOR PUBLIC RELEASE**

## Repository boundary

PETRA VISION work may exist only in repositories whose private status
has been verified before publication of each branch.

## Prohibited public disclosure

Do not place PETRA VISION technical material in:

- public repositories;
- public issues or pull requests;
- public commit messages;
- profile update generators;
- public CI artifacts;
- package registries;
- screenshots or demonstrations;
- conference, social, forum, or chat publications;
- public AI conversation links.

## Commit-message discipline

Private commit messages should remain technically useful but avoid
unnecessary disclosure in tooling that may aggregate activity.

Public profile generators must continue excluding private
repositories.

## Third-party access

Before sharing enabling technical detail with an external person or
organisation:

1. identify the minimum information required;
2. establish the purpose of disclosure;
3. use an appropriate confidentiality agreement;
4. record who received which material and when;
5. avoid public or uncontrolled communication channels.

## Generated artifacts

Rendered geometries, diagrams, benchmark corpora, videos, notebooks,
and exported documents are confidential research artifacts unless
explicitly cleared after professional IP review.

## External services

Do not upload enabling technical artifacts to external services
without confirming their confidentiality, retention, and training
policies.

## Publication gate

No PETRA VISION technical publication is authorised until one of
these decisions is recorded:

- a patent application has been filed and publication is approved;
- professional review concludes that publication is acceptable;
- the project is deliberately retained as a trade secret;
- the confidentiality strategy is formally abandoned.
